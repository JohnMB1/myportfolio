My Story

My name is John Moses Bahago. I am a process-oriented Petroleum Engineering graduate and distance learning student with a strong passion for data validation, operational leadership, and sustainable community development.

I enjoy coordinating multi-stakeholder workflows, optimizing technical processes, and driving team excellence in high-pressure environments. My goal is to leverage my analytical training and administrative competencies to solve real-world operational challenges while continuously evolving as a leader.

The move into Data Science isn't a departure from my engineering roots — it's an extension of them. The same mindset I applied to subsurface analysis and reservoir evaluation now drives how I approach data: with rigour, curiosity, and a genuine desire to find the signal in the noise.

Career Aspirations

My long-term goal is to operate at the intersection of data science and energy sector operations — a space that's still largely under-explored in the African context. I want to build tools and frameworks that help energy companies make smarter decisions, faster, using real-time data analytics and predictive modelling.

  • Become a certified Data Scientist with a focus on energy analytics
  • Build AI-assisted tools for reservoir management and production forecasting
  • Lead cross-functional teams in operational excellence initiatives
  • Contribute to community-driven data literacy programmes in Nigeria
